Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Short version: we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. Your game save is stored on your phone and online, under a guest account with no name. The game contains no advertising and does not track you across apps. We do not sell any data.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Felly Lunkeba, sole trader (French micro-entreprise), 77 avenue des Jonquilles, 91800 Boussy-Saint-Antoine, France, SIRET 791 754 682 00015.

For any question about your data: genghis27000@gmail.com.

We do not have a data protection officer: the law does not require us to have one.

2. What the game stores on your phone

  • Your year of birth (requested at first launch): to know whether you are an adult. Under 18, purchases, offers, notifications and partners are closed. Legal basis: our obligation to protect minors.
  • Your game save: dice, cash, properties, worlds, album, missions, tournament, tokens, settings. Legal basis: performance of the contract (the terms of use).
  • The record of your purchases (date, product, price), visible in “My purchases”. Legal basis: performance of the contract.
  • Your notification choices and the reminder schedule. Legal basis: your consent (the phone's permission).
  • The graphics detail level chosen for your device. Legal basis: performance of the contract.

Your year of birth is required to play. Nothing else is asked of you.

3. Purchases

Purchases go through your phone's store (Google Play or the App Store). We never receive your card number, your name or your address. The store only sends us what is needed to deliver the purchase: the product identifier, an order number and an encrypted receipt. The store processes your payment data under its own responsibility:

  • Google Play: Google Ireland Limited and Google Commerce Limited (Ireland) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • App Store: Apple Distribution International Limited (Ireland) — apple.com/legal/privacy

Accounting records of sales (order number, date, product, amount, country) are kept for ten years, as required by the French Commercial Code (art. L. 123-22). They contain nothing from your game save.

Personalized offers. We use your stage in the game and your in-game purchase history (last 30 days) to automatically choose which offers are shown to you, and when. These offers bear the label “Personalized offer”. You can object to this in SETTINGS (“Personalized offers”): offers will then no longer depend on your purchases.

4. Notifications

Reminders are prepared by your phone itself. You accept them or not when the game asks you, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → “Notifications”, or in your phone's settings. Never more than 4 per day, none between 10 pm and 8 am, none if you are a minor.

5. Your account and your online save

At first launch, the game creates a guest account for you: a simple identifier, with no name, no address and no password. It is used to keep your game save online (your progress, your dice, your cash, your album, your year of birth) and the register of your purchases, so that they can be restored to you if you change phones and so that a purchase is never delivered twice or lost. Legal basis: performance of the contract; fraud prevention (our legitimate interest).

In the shared tournament, other players see your player number, your avatar, your token and your score, nothing else; your Private Circle frame only if you have chosen to show it.

To operate and protect the service, our hosting provider also processes your IP address, your device model and the time of exchanges.

This data is stored on our behalf by Unity (Unity Technologies), our processor; it may be hosted in the United States, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. The game's remote settings are the same for everyone and receive no profile data.

6. Your partners

If you open partners between players, we keep: your partner name (chosen from our lists), your partner code, the list of your partners, your requests and the players you have blocked, who invited you and whom you invited, your sticker trades and gifts (90 days), your reports and those concerning you (1 year). Your partners see your partner name, your avatar, your token, your world, your net worth, whether you have played today or this week, and your duplicate stickers; never your purchases. Legal basis: performance of the contract; prevention of cheating (our legitimate interest). The game never reads your address book. These features are closed to accounts declared as minors. When you delete your game save, all of this is deleted.

7. Who receives data

  • Your phone's store, for purchases (section 3).
  • Unity, our processor, for the account, the save, the tournament and partners (sections 5 and 6).
  • Other players, only what sections 5 and 6 state.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires us to.

No one else. No advertising network, no data broker. Rivals and opponents labelled “Character” are played by the game: none of your data is sent to them.

8. How long

  • Your game save, your year of birth, your settings, your account and your online save: until you tap “Delete my game”.
  • “My purchases” record: the current month and the previous month.
  • Accounting records of sales: 10 years.
  • Sticker trades: 90 days; reports: 1 year.
  • Your messages to genghis27000@gmail.com: 3 years after the last exchange.

Important: as long as your guest account has not been restored on another device, it exists only on this phone. If you uninstall the game without having noted your recovery code (Settings → “My account”), we cannot restore your game save.

9. Your rights

At any time, free of charge, you can:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • have it rectified (your year of birth can be corrected once in Settings → “Info & legal”);
  • have it erased: Settings → “My account” → “Delete my game” deletes your account, your online save and everything the game has written on your phone; without the game: https://lebestial243.github.io/goldenascent/suppression.html;
  • withdraw your consent (notifications) as easily as you gave it;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, or request its restriction;
  • request the portability of the data you have provided to us;
  • give instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death.

Write to genghis27000@gmail.com with your player ID (Settings → “My account”). We reply within one month. We never ask for an identity document.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL: cnil.fr/fr/plaintes, or CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07. If you live in another country of the Union, you may lodge a complaint with that country's authority.

10. Minors

The game is designed for adults. If your year of birth indicates you are under 18: no purchases, no offers, no notifications, no partners. A parent can write to us at genghis27000@gmail.com.

11. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage, protected by your phone. Exchanges with our hosting provider are encrypted (HTTPS).

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is displayed in the game with its date. If a change affects your consent, we will ask for it again.
