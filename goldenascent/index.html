Golden Ascent — Support

Golden Ascent is a dice board game published by Felly Lunkeba (sole trader).

Contact us

genghis27000@gmail.com: we answer within one month at most, usually much sooner. If your question is about your game, include your player ID (Settings → "My account").

FAQ

  • A purchase did not arrive: open the SHOP and tap "RESTORE PURCHASES" at the very bottom. Otherwise, write to us with the date of the purchase.
  • Refunds are requested from the store. App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: your order history.
  • New phone: Settings → "My account" gives you what you need to get your game back.
  • Delete your game and your data: Settings → "My account" → "Delete my game", or the "Delete my data" page above.
